Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyNaijaDiet.

Is the meal plan generation really free? expand_more

Yes! Setting up your profile, tracking your TDEE, and generating your weekly Nigerian meal plans are completely free to use.

Do I have to cook everything from scratch? expand_more

Not at all. The AI recommends standard Nigerian meals. You can cook them yourself, eat them at a local restaurant, or buy them from a vendor, as long as you maintain the recommended portion sizes.

How accurate is the AI model? expand_more

Our recommendation engine is built on a LightGBM machine learning framework trained on over 300+ unique Nigerian meals, prioritizing precise macro-nutrient matching for your specific body data.
